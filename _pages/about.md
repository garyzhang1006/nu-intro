Hi, I'm Gary. I'm a student researcher interested in machine learning for scientific computing — in particular, using graph neural networks to learn mesh-based physics simulations and exploring adversarial training (GANs) to sharpen long-rollout predictions.

Outside of research, I build software. Recent projects include [GreenPath](https://github.com/wwhaiming/greenpath), an AI-assisted guide for navigating the US green card process; [EcoRise](https://github.com/wwhaiming/EcoRise), an app that lets people scan their activities to track CO2 emissions, wrapped in a gamified leaderboard; and an interactive browser visualization of learned fluid dynamics on unstructured meshes.

This site collects my [projects](/projects/), occasional [notes](/blog/), and a [CV](/cv/). To make it yours: replace this bio, drop a photo named `prof_pic.jpg` into `assets/img/`, edit `_data/socials.yml` for the icons below, and fill in `_bibliography/papers.bib` if you have publications.
