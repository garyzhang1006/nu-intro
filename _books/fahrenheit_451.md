Bradbury's dystopia where firemen burn books instead of putting out fires. What stuck with me is that the censorship does not start with the state. It starts with people wanting to be comfortable, so the books go quietly before anyone is forced to give them up. Montag's slow turn from burning books to memorizing them is the whole argument in one character.
